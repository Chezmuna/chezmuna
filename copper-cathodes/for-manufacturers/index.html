Why Quality Consistency Matters Here

Cathode quality flows straight into rod quality

A continuous-cast rod mill or cable line is sensitive to cathode consistency in a way a general trading buyer isn't — impurity variance shows up downstream as conductivity and drawability issues. That's why the specification here is fixed and verified at two points before it ever reaches a mill: warehouse verification at origin, then independent SGS inspection at load port.
