Who Is Who

Where Chezmuna sits in the chain

Chezmuna Enterprises acts as authorized sales agent for Misk Investments Limited (Kenya), the mandate seller, which holds the commercial mandate for a DRC copper producer. The producer's identity is confidential under the terms of the mandate. The structure is stated openly: Chezmuna presents the seller's offer and procedure to buyers; the seller issues the FCO and enters into the SPA; and the cargo is independently verified at defined stages, so buyers do not have to rely on seller statements alone.

Origin, storage, and load port

Cathodes are held at a bonded warehouse in Kinshasa, where a buyer or their representative may verify the existence and condition of the cargo before any financial instrument is opened. From there, cargo moves to load port at Dar es Salaam or Mombasa for SGS inspection and shipment. Transit and export documentation arrangements are confirmed in the FCO and SPA.

Common Questions

Before you write in

What does "Grade-A copper cathode" mean?

Refined copper cathode at 99.97% minimum purity, produced to a recognized standard — here, ASTM B115 Grade 1 or GB/T 467-2010 Cu-CATH-1 — suitable for wire rod, cable, and tube manufacturing.

What is Chezmuna's role?

Chezmuna Enterprises is the authorized sales agent for Misk Investments Limited, which holds the commercial sales mandate for a DRC copper producer. Chezmuna presents the seller's offer and procedure to buyers; final terms are set out in the FCO and SPA.

Why isn't the producer named?

Producer identity is confidential under the sales mandate and the non-circumvention and non-disclosure (NCND) clause in the SPA. Buyers can still verify the transaction independently: cargo is inspected at the Kinshasa bonded warehouse and by SGS at load port, and funds move only through a UCP 600 bank instrument.

Can I see documents from past transactions?

No. The SPA contains an NCND clause, so documents relating to past or other transactions and counterparties are not disclosed. Pre-instrument cargo verification is welcome, however: after the SPA is executed and before the financial instrument is opened, you or your representative may verify the designated cargo at the Kinshasa bonded warehouse.

Can I verify the cargo before opening an LC?

Yes. After the SPA is executed and before any financial instrument is opened, the buyer or its representative may verify the designated cargo at the Kinshasa bonded warehouse. Independent SGS inspection at the load port follows, in accordance with the SPA.

Which banking instruments and issuing banks are acceptable?

Settlement is through an ICC UCP 600 DLC/LC between top-tier banks. If your bank requires a different structure, a confirmation or a performance bond, tell us in your enquiry so it can be reviewed with the seller before an LOI. Final terms are agreed in the SPA.

Is the product an LME-registered brand?

No. Pricing is negotiated against the LME Cash Settlement reference at a discount fixed in the sale and purchase agreement, rather than tied to a deliverable LME warrant.

What happens if my mandatory requirements differ from the published procedure?

Tell us in your enquiry. We review material differences with the seller before inviting an LOI, so neither side spends weeks on a transaction that cannot proceed under the agreed procedure.

Ready to start with a buyer enquiry?

Tell us your requirements and we'll review them against the seller's procedure and advise on the next step.
