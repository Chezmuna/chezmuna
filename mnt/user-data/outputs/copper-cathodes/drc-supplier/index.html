Origin, storage, and load port

Cathodes are held at a bonded warehouse in Kinshasa, where a buyer or their representative may verify the existence and condition of the cargo before any financial instrument is opened. From there, cargo moves to load port at Dar es Salaam or Mombasa for SGS inspection and shipment.

Common Questions

Before you write in

What does "Grade-A copper cathode" mean?

Refined copper cathode at 99.95% minimum purity, produced to a recognized standard — here, ASTM B115 Grade 1 or GB/T 467-2010 Cu-CATH-1 — suitable for wire rod, cable, and tube manufacturing.

Is Chezmuna a broker?

No. Chezmuna is authorized sales agent for the mandate holder of the DRC mine. Negotiation and contracting run principal-to-principal, not through a broker chain.

Can I verify the cargo before opening an LC?

Yes. Warehouse verification in Kinshasa happens before any financial instrument is opened, followed by independent SGS inspection at load port.
