Verification, Not Claims

Buyer Verification Before Financial Commitment

Qualified buyers are welcome to verify the transaction before opening the financial instrument. Following execution of the SPA, the buyer or its authorized representative may verify the designated copper cathodes at the Kinshasa bonded warehouse, subject to the agreed procedure, to confirm physical availability and transaction details. Independent SGS inspection follows at the designated load port in accordance with the SPA.

Because documents relating to other transactions and counterparties are covered by the NCND clause in the SPA, the buyer's main assurance is physical. Cargo verification takes place after the SPA and before any financial commitment, and quality and quantity are confirmed again at loading.

Enquiry Review → Buyer LOI → Seller FCO → SPA → Buyer Pre-Inspection → Financial Instrument → SGS Inspection → Shipment

Before You Submit a Letter of Intent

Confirm transaction compatibility before you proceed

We encourage prospective buyers to establish their technical, commercial, verification and banking requirements before submitting a formal LOI. This lets your procurement, compliance and banking teams confirm early that the transaction can work for them, and it saves both sides time. Please tell us:

Specification & quantity

Tell us your required copper purity, technical standard, trial quantity, anticipated monthly volume and destination port.

What your compliance team needs

Tell us what your compliance team must see before proceeding. Documents relating to other transactions and counterparties are not disclosed under the NCND clause in the SPA, so we can confirm early what the procedure can and cannot accommodate.

Cargo verification

Pre-instrument cargo verification is welcome. After the SPA and before the financial instrument is opened, you may verify the designated cargo at the Kinshasa bonded warehouse, followed by SGS at load port.

Banking & payment

Indicate your proposed instrument, issuing bank, any confirmation or performance-bond expectations, and the conditions under which payment would fall due.

Delivery terms

Specify your preferred Incoterm, loading and destination ports, and any other essential commercial conditions.

How we proceed

We review your enquiry, identify any material differences between your requirements and the established transaction procedure, and refer them to the seller where necessary. Where the requirements are compatible, qualified buyers are invited to submit a formal LOI for consideration.

Submitting an enquiry is not an LOI, an offer, a commitment to sell, or a guarantee that every requested term or document will be accepted. Final commercial terms, documentation and verification arrangements are subject to seller approval and the executed transaction documents.
