Two Trade Lines

Our Divisions

Copper cathode facilitation and industrial raw material sourcing are run as separate desks, each with its own buyers, documentation, and process.

Grade-A DRC Copper Cathodes

Direct facilitation for Non-LME Registered Electrolytic Copper Cathodes, 99.99%–99.999% purity, sourced via authorized mandate from Shire Mines SPRL (DRC).

  • 1,000 MT trial cargoes; up to 10,000 MT/month rolling contracts
  • Pre-DLC physical inspection via SGS / Alex Stewart
  • CIF/FOB delivery via Dar es Salaam, Durban, Beira
  • ICC UCP 600 DLC/LC settlement

Standard Process

  1. 1Buyer issues LOI / ICPO
  2. 2Seller issues FCO; both principals execute SPA
  3. 3Buyer's technical team inspects cargo on-site (Kinshasa)
  4. 4Buyer's bank issues DLC under UCP 600
  5. 5Export clearance, port assay, vessel loading
  6. 6Bank-to-bank settlement against shipping/analytical docs
